# Berlin Electronic Music Labels

## Ostgut Ton

Founded 2002. The in-house label of Berghain, Berlin's most important techno club. Releases music exclusively by artists associated with the club — Ben Klock, Marcel Dettmann, Shed, Fiedel, Steffi, Virginia, Nick Höppner, Cassy. The sound: dark, functional, undecorated techno and house. No remixes by outside artists. Highly selective catalogue. Distributed by Word and Sound. Every release is a significant event in Berlin techno.

## Tresor Records

Founded 1991 alongside the club. The original Detroit-Berlin exchange. Early releases by Juan Atkins (Model 500), Blake Baxter, Robert Hood, Jeff Mills. Later: Surgeon, Planetary Assault Systems (Luke Slater), Truncate. Still active, still a reference point for hard techno and Detroit-influenced electronics.

## BPitch Control

Founded 1999 by DJ and producer Ellen Allien. More eclectic than Ostgut Ton — house, techno, electro, and experimental electronics. Key artists: Paul Kalkbrenner (Berlin Calling), Sascha Funke, Modeselektor, Apparat. Less active in recent years but historically one of Berlin's most globally distributed labels.

## Innervisions

Founded by Dixon and Âme (Frank Wiedemann + Kristian Beyer). Based in Berlin. The label for melodic, emotional, European deep house and house-adjacent techno. Key artists: Dixon, Âme, Ryan Crosson, Stimming, Trikk, Henrik Schwarz. Watergate crowd. Releases are typically 12" singles or EPs; distribution via global networks. The sound is warm, progressive, and narrative — stories told in dance music.

## Klockworks

Ben Klock's personal label. Strictly dark, hard Berlin techno. No decoration, no compromise. Ben Klock, Stef Mendesidis, Voices From The Lake. Each release reinforces the Klockworks aesthetic: functional, long, midnight-to-morning music.

## MDR (Marcel Dettmann Records)

Marcel Dettmann's label. Similar ethos to Klockworks: honest, straight-ahead techno. Marcel Dettmann, Blawan, Shed. Not prolific — each release is carefully considered.

## Stroboscopic Artefacts

Founded by Lucy (Luca Mortellaro). Berlin. More experimental and cerebral than the Berghain axis — influences from modern composition, noise, and conceptual art. Lucy, Dadub, Rrose, Xhin. Less dancefloor-functional, more gallery or listening room.

## Perlon

Founded 1996 in Frankfurt but long associated with the Berlin minimal scene. Ricardo Villalobos, Zip, Markus Nicolai, Sammy Dee. The label for deep, understated minimal house/techno hybrids. Releases are deliberately obscure and collector-oriented.

## Basic Channel / Chain Reaction

Not technically a Berlin club label but foundational to Berlin's sound. Mark Ernestus and Moritz von Oswald invented dub techno in Berlin in the early 1990s under the Basic Channel name and the Chain Reaction sublabel. The sound — slow, reverb-drenched, bass-heavy, hypnotic — influenced everything from Tresor to Berghain.

## Other notable active labels

- **Ternesc** — Blawan. Industrial and experimental.
- **Dystopian** — Dax J, Alignment, Rebekah. Hard, dark, festival techno.
- **Fabric / hessle audio** — not Berlin but essential UK techno/bass labels with strong Berlin following.
