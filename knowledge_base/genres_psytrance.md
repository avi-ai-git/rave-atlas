# Psytrance (Psychedelic Trance)

## What it is

Psychedelic trance (psytrance) evolved from Goa trance in the early 1990s, originating in the beach party scene of Goa, India, where Western travellers mixed synthesiser music with psychedelic culture. It emphasised altered states, spiritual symbolism, and marathon outdoor raves over conventional club settings.

## BPM and rhythmic signature

- **BPM range:** 138-148 BPM for full-on and progressive psytrance; dark/forest psytrance can reach 148-155.
- **Kick pattern:** Heavy, punchy four-on-the-floor kick, often with a strong subbass layer.
- **Character:** Layered, hypnotic, with rapid arpeggio sequences on synthesisers and a consistent "rolling" forward motion. The signature sound is a buzzing, modulated synthesiser line that weaves around the kick.

## How to recognise it

- The "rolling" synth arpeggio, a rapidly repeating melodic pattern that evolves slowly over many bars.
- Reverb-heavy, psychedelic production: sounds feel wide and spacious, often with alien or otherworldly textures.
- Lacks the industrial coldness of techno; has more colour and melodic warmth but is faster and more hypnotic than house.
- Commonly heard at outdoor festivals (Fusion in Germany, Boom in Portugal) rather than indoor clubs.

## Key subgenres

- **Full-on:** Uplifting, melodic, festival-friendly. Infected Mushroom, Astrix.
- **Dark / forest psytrance:** Twisted, dystopian, late-night. Ott, Kindzadza.
- **Progressive psytrance:** More subtle, layered, closer to progressive trance. Atmos, Filteria.
- **Goa trance:** The origin. More melodic, cosmic. Juno Reactor, Man With No Name.

## Key artists and labels

Infected Mushroom, Astrix, Arjuna, Juno Reactor, Ott. Labels: Hadra Records, Iono Music, TIP Records.

## Berlin context

Psytrance is not a primary Berlin club genre but has a strong festival presence in Germany. The Fusion Festival (Lärz, Brandenburg) is one of Europe's most important psytrance and experimental electronic music gatherings. Berlin's SO36 and occasional outdoor events in Tempelhof or Tempelhofer Feld host psytrance nights.
