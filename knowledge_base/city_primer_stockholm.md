# Stockholm Electronic Music Scene

Stockholm's electronic scene emerged from Swedish House Mafia's global success but maintains a serious underground techno and minimal culture. The city's waterfront warehouses and industrial spaces host quality bookings despite strict alcohol laws and high costs. Strong local production talent exports to Berlin and beyond.

## Key Venues
- **Slakthuset**: Former slaughterhouse in Johanneshov, raw industrial space, Under Bron brand hosts serious techno sessions.
- **Farbror Nikos**: Intimate basement club, quality house and techno.
- **Sodra Teatern**: Historic venue with electronic nights, beautiful views over the city.
- **Under Bron**: Brand that hosts events at various warehouses and industrial spaces.

## Sound and Vibe
Minimal, deep house, and hypnotic techno dominate the underground, while commercial venues push mainstream EDM. Swedish producers (Adam Beyer, Axel Boman, Petter) influence the sound. Very expensive (25-30 EUR entry, 8-10 EUR drinks), strict licensing (alcohol sales stop at specific times). Serious crowd, less touristy than Berlin or Amsterdam.
