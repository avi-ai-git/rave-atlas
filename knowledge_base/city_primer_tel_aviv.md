# Tel Aviv Electronic Music Scene

Tel Aviv's 24/7 culture, Mediterranean beaches, and Middle Eastern energy create a unique electronic music environment. The city's progressive values and party-all-the-time ethos fuel a scene that blends techno, house, and psytrance with local flavors. Often called "the city that never stops," Tel Aviv's clubs and beaches host marathon sessions.

## Key Venues
- **The Block**: Industrial warehouse complex, serious techno bookings, multiple rooms.
- **Alphabet**: Former printing house, quality house and techno.
- **Bootleg**: Underground club, intimate setting.
- **Breakfast Club**: Outdoor venue, daytime and nighttime parties.
- **Kuli Alma**: Bar and venue hosting electronic nights.
- **Beach parties**: Hilton Beach and others host impromptu and organized parties year-round.

## Sound and Vibe
Techno, house, and psytrance (Israel's Goa heritage) blend with Middle Eastern influences. The scene's intensity reflects Israeli work-hard-party-hard culture. Thursday-Saturday nights are massive (Friday is like Western Saturday). Affordable, friendly door policies, welcoming international DJs. Summer beach parties are legendary. 24-hour culture means parties run anytime.
