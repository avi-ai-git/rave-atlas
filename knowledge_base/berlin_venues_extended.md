# Berlin Venues: Mid-Size and Smaller Clubs, Neighbourhoods

## Sisyphos Overview (Rummelsburg, Lichtenberg)

Sisyphos occupies a former dog-biscuit factory (Hundekuchenfabrik) in Rummelsburger Bucht, Lichtenberg. Sommerleuchten e.V. took over the site in 2008, with first parties in 2009. Operator is Sisyphos Event GmbH; founding managing directors were Julius Hausl and Lina Thiele. Capacity with the outdoor area is about 1,500. Parties run every weekend from spring to New Year, typically Friday 22:00 continuously to Monday 10:00, though only the Wintergarten floor runs continuously. The club takes a winter pause from after New Year to roughly early March. Entry is usually around 15 euros, sometimes 20 to 25, paid once for the whole weekend.

### Sisyphos Floors and Named Spaces

- Hammahalle: the main warehouse hall, "the mothership," steel and concrete, big sound system, harder techno. Opens later, fills fast.
- Wintergarten: small chalet near the entrance, "the living room," open all night, fills from around 01:30, lighter house, electro, disco.
- Dampfer: robust floor with a springy wooden floor.
- Strand and Sonnendeck: outdoor beach area with a kiosk, abandoned cars, cocktail bar and festival feel.
- Tunnel: additional space listed on the venue's unofficial timetable.

### Sisyphos Recurring Formats and What to Know

- Lineups are almost never announced in advance; the schedule arrives last minute. Fan tools sisy.fan and sisyduck.com track floor timetables.
- Named multi-day weekenders recur under rotating banners (for example "Rolling Again," Friday to Monday). The marathon format is the core ritual.
- Residents include Foolik and Anna Schreit.
- Crowd: average age 25 to 30, very international, mixed house, electro and techno.
- Dress: relaxed, casual, colourful, festival-leaning; not all-black. Do not overdress.
- Door: difficult because demand exceeds capacity, with long queues all night and terse bouncers who do not explain refusals. Dress down and know it is a rave.
- Ticket: door-based, cash. Long cloakroom lines.
- Getting there: about 2 km east of Ostkreuz; walk, taxi (about 10 euros from Ostkreuz), or Tram 21 to Gustav-Holzmann-Strasse.

## Kater (Holzmarkt, Friedrichshain)

Now named Kater (formerly Kater Blau, before that Kater Holzig, descended from Bar25). Located at Holzmarktstrasse 25 on the Spree on the Holzmarkt 25 collective grounds. Operator is Kater Club GmbH (founded 2014); managing director Christoph Klenzendorf co-founded the predecessors. Indoor capacity about 200; with the outdoor area and the "Agnes" boat, up to about 800. Reach via S-Bahn (Ostbahnhof or Jannowitzbrücke) or U8 (Jannowitzbrücke).

### Kater Recurring Formats and What to Know

- SaSoMo (Samstag-Sonntag-Montag): officially continuous from Friday-to-Saturday around midnight through Monday morning, with a lineup break Saturday afternoon and evening.
- Katernacht: the recurring Saturday-into-weekend night, listed regularly on RA (September and October 2025 editions documented).
- KaterWeekend: the longer recurring weekend format.
- Agnes boat: the wooden boat moored outside, serving as a terrace and extra dancefloor in season.
- Floors: Heinz Hopper (main, techno all the time, leftfield spotlights) and Acid Arc / Acid Bogen (smaller, alternative, ambient, live). A "hangover kiosk" (Kater Holzig) behind the entrance sells sweets and drinks; DJs play there by day.
- Residents include Dirty Doering (carried from Bar25).
- Crowd: mixed, glitter ravers next to black-clad techno fans and tourists; relatively relaxed door.
- Dress: explicitly NOT all-black; colour, glitter and creativity encouraged.
- Door: bouncers select strictly; stay relaxed if refused. Some high New Year prices reported (around 40 euros).
- Ticket: limited presale on RA, more at the club; presale does not guarantee entry.

## ://about blank (Friedrichshain, near Ostkreuz)

Opened 2010 by a collective in a former GDR transport technical office at Markgrafendamm 24c. Two indoor floors plus a large garden that becomes an open-air floor in warm weather; summer parties run several days. Openly anti-Deutsch politics, which drove party splits. Cash only; phone cameras must be covered. Entry usually 12 to 15 euros; a stamp allows return. Pizza stand out front. At Ostkreuz.

### ://about blank Recurring Parties

- Homopatik: queer party that ran the third Friday monthly, close to 24 hours. It split: resident Mr. Ties kept the name; co-founder Danilo Rosato continued at the venue as Buttons.
- Buttons: queer party, monthly Friday from 23:59, since 2016, successor to Homopatik. Pushing house and techno, three floors, open-air in summer, garden-sex exploration, water-bomb games. In June 2021 Buttons announced it would leave ://about blank over the club's pro-Israel stance, as part of Berlin Nightlife Workers Against Apartheid.
- Staub: monthly Saturday daytime techno, 10:00 to 22:00, secret lineup never announced, run by the people behind I/Y.
- Room 4 Resistance: bi-monthly, queer and political; the club cut ties in 2018 after it supported #DJsForPalestine.
- Golosa: queer party, two rooms (house, then techno and acid), careful door to keep a safe queer space.
- Garden parties: the outdoor garden floor hosts open-air daytime parties in summer, especially Homopatik/Buttons and Staub daytimers.
- Door tip: do not arrive as an obvious bachelor group or in novelty techno tourist shirts. Dark tones, dusty-ready sneakers. Pro-LGBTQ+, anti-fascist space.

## Salon zur Wilden Renate / Wilde Renate (Alt-Stralau) -- CLOSED 2025

Opened in an abandoned apartment block in Friedrichshain in 2007; announced permanent closure in 2025 after 18 years. Address Alt-Stralau 70. Three floors (Black, Green, Red), the Absinth room, a courtyard, maze-like apartment decor. Documented for the historical record because its parties seeded the wider scene.

### Wilde Renate Recurring Series (Historical)

- Savour The Moment: marathon party by Berlin DJ Marlon Hoffstadt, debuted February 2018. Phone-free ethos: leave your phone in the cloakroom, no photos or videos, ask the DJ for a track ID instead of Shazaming. Sets running over 30 hours. Lineups featured Ciel, Prosumer, K-Hand, D.Tiffany, Moxie, Juju and Jordash, DJ Assault, Detroit In Effect, Ash Lauryn. Renate was a new entry at Position 94 in the DJ Mag Top 100 Clubs poll 2019.
- Pretty/Ugly: long-running diversity-forward party, residents Peak and Swift and DJ TEETH, with an explicit anti-racist, anti-sexist, anti-homophobic, anti-transphobic dancefloor statement.
- Other named nights: Floppy Drive, Liquid, Jetzt, House of Red Doors (quarterly, at sister venue Else).
- "IN RENATE WE TRUST": the door mantra; RA tickets never guaranteed entry.
- The team announced a new club, M01.

## Club der Visionaere (Flutgraben, between Kreuzberg and Treptow)

Open-air canal-side institution at the Flutgraben of the Landwehrkanal, debuted 2002. Small brick building, tiny indoor dancefloor (under 50), wooden deck under a weeping willow, jetty over the water. More an afterhours bar than a club; entry usually 5 euros or less, sometimes free. Quiet but clear sound. Cash only. Pizzeria upstairs in summer.

### Club der Visionaere Recurring Patterns

- Booking: deliberately relaxed and spontaneous; lineup decided one to two weeks ahead, unplanned sets common. Big weekends run Friday through Monday without a break.
- Seasonal: traditionally summer-only, closed in winter, open daily in summer from the afternoon. The roof is now sealed with furnace heaters for some cold-season parties; fire-regulation issues sometimes move winter parties to the Hoppetosse boat or the small "Sonnenraum."
- Sound: minimal techno and house dominate; vinyl-leaning. Past guests Richie Hawtin and Ricardo Villalobos; regulars Federico Molinari and Jan Krueger.
- Affiliated: Hoppetosse (boat, winter home), Rosengarten, Anhalt, Bootshaus, Rhein. Recovered from a 2019 fire. Part of the Arena Berlin complex.

## OHM (Mitte, in Kraftwerk next to Tresor)

OHM occupies the former battery room of Kraftwerk Berlin at Kopenicker Strasse 70, adjacent to Tresor. Small, intimate, low-ceilinged industrial space. Sound leans experimental, ambient, electro and techno. TRADE put on event nights here from 2015. It is a regular stage for Berlin Atonal festival nights and hosted parts of the Tresor 31 anniversary series in 2021. Genre-agnostic DJ nights and label and collective showcases recur.

## Tresor (Mitte, Kopenicker Strasse 70, Kraftwerk)

Founded March 1991 in the vault of the former Wertheim department store on Leipziger Strasse; lineage from the UFO club (1988) and founder Dimitri Hegemann. Closed April 2005, reopened 24 May 2007 in Kraftwerk Berlin. Tresor Records founded October 1991 (Jeff Mills, Robert Hood, Drexciya, Surgeon, Juan Atkins). Floors: the basement Tresor vault (hard Detroit techno via a long tunnel), the main battery room / Globus floor (house and electro), and +4 Bar. Fair, easy door. Tresor.West in Dortmund since November 2019.

### Tresor Recurring Formats

- Tresor Klubnacht: the recurring Saturday club night.
- New Faces: recurring Wednesday series for emerging artists, often guest-curated (BCCO, Maedon).
- Tresor meets / Tresor invites: recurring label collaborations (No Way Back, OURA Records, Sonic Groove, James Ruskin / Blueprint).
- Singularity: recurring Monday format.
- Herrensauna: queer techno party, now typically Fridays (see collectives).

## KitKatClub (Mitte, Kopenicker Strasse 76)

Opened March 1994 by Austrian filmmaker Simon Thaur and partner Kirsten Kruger. Famously sex-positive; motto "do what you want but stay in communication." Three floors plus an outdoor pool, sauna, candy shop and playspaces. Floors: Main Floor, Dragon Floor, Prisma Bar. Intimate play areas with beds above the floors. 30th anniversary 2 March 2024. U8 to Heinrich-Heine-Strasse.

### KitKatClub Recurring Nights

- CarneBall Bizarre: flagship, every Saturday, all floors open. Rolls into the Nachspiel afterhour on Sunday without re-entry or re-payment. Dress: kinky, fetish, gothic, latex, leather, costumes, glitter, glamour, elegant; effort required, colour encouraged. Often door-checked by Kirsten Kruger.
- Symbiotikka: every Wednesday, hypnotic techno, dark and intimate, mainly Main Floor; extra floors open for big names (Stella Bossi). One of Berlin's best midweek parties. Photographer Gili Shani shoots it.
- Nachspiel: Sunday afterhour from around 07:00 to 08:00 into the afternoon, calmer, pool hangout. Dress: burlesque, eye-catching, fetish, sexy.
- Electric Monday: Monday, deep house and techno, relaxed but still KitKat.
- Piep Show: "sporty, colourful, kinky, sexy, fetish, creative, costumes."
- Four Play / Fourplay: first Friday monthly, newcomer-friendly, pioneered colour in kink dress codes.
- Gegen: queer party hosted here (see collectives).
- Historical: Kitty Goldmine, Revolver Party, HustlaBall Peepshow.
- Nudity is permitted and common but not required; arriving undressed does not guarantee entry.

## Ritter Butzke (Kreuzberg, Ritterstrasse 26, near Moritzplatz)

Started February 2007 as an illegal club in the Butzke-Werke factory halls; reopened with permission at Ritterstrasse 26 in October 2009. Up to four floors plus a courtyard chill-out and a summer open-air floor; melodic, happier techno, house, deep house, minimal, electro. Disco balls, chandeliers, hanging plants. Has Ritter Butzke Records. Deposit 1 euro for glasses, 50 cents for bottles and cans. Best nights Thursday to Saturday. 18+.

### Ritter Butzke Residents and Series

- Residents and regulars: Andi De Luxe, Aroma, Daniel Solar, Don Brazo, Gutzeit and Wandel, Heimlich Knuller, Jan Mir, Jens Bond, Marc Poppcke, Sven Dohse, David Dorad, Kotelett and Zadak. Label duo Prismode and Solvane head Ritter Butzke Records (progressive and melodic techno).
- Named series: Sender Freies Butzke, Lila, Im a Beatfreak, Monkee's Madness, Burlesque Musique, Steyoyoke, Format:B nights, "Unter freiem Himmel" free-entry open-air nights.
- Door: unforgiving despite no formal dress code; buy online to skip the ticket line.

## Anomalie Art Club (Prenzlauer Berg, Storkower Strasse 123) -- also branded DSTRKT

Arty alternative club; industrial charm, outdoor area. Open some Fridays 23:00 to Saturdays 10:00 for special events; cost around 20 euros. Relatively easygoing door for Berlin, laid-back dress, though refusals reported. Check RA for event-specific tickets.

### Anomalie Recurring Nights

- Teletech Berlin: the major recurring techno, trance, house and minimal night (promoter Polka Bookings), 23:00 to 10:00, with large lineups (Vladimir Dubyshkin, Franck, DJ Hyperdrive). Hard and fast techno and trance crowd; very large attendance.
- Art exhibitions, film screenings and other techno nights round out the program. Runs an Art and Beer Garden in summer.

## Else (Alt-Treptow, An den Treptowers 10)

Open-air sister of Wilde Renate, by the Elsenbrucke where Kreuzberg, Treptow, Friedrichshain and Alt-Stralau meet, on the Spree. Sun sails, light installations, communal daytime feel with pizza and beer. Mixed-age crowd, less all-black than typical Berlin.

### Else Recurring Formats

- Hansel and Brezel: weekday biergarten incarnation, Tuesdays to Saturdays from 14:00, beer, pretzels, Bavarian Obazda and Bergkase with music. Pils on tap (4 euros), pretzels with homemade Obazda (5 euros), Weisswurst, oak casks of Bavarian Helles. An "alternative Oktoberfest" each late September.
- Sunday dance wind-downs are a signature.
- House of Red Doors: extravagant quarterly bash from the Renate family.
- Marlon Hoffstadt's Savour The Moment marathons were key to Else and Renate.
- Else, Renate, ://about blank and Neukolln venues have faced threat from the A100 motorway extension.

## Birgit und Bier (Kreuzberg, Schleusenufer, by the canal)

Open-air location, beer garden and club on the bank of the Kreuzberg locks; fairground atmosphere with bumper cars, a carousel, table football and skittles. Four floors in club mode in the evenings; techno, pizza and beer by day.

- SONAR / Safer Nightlife Berlin: Birgit and Bier hosts the SONAR safer-nightlife project, an association of Berlin prevention and addiction-support projects with the Clubcommission. SONAR runs info stands, harm-reduction workshops and individual drug counselling at events, with a non-judgmental approach. Partners include Fixpunkt, Vista, Notdienst, Clubcommission and Eclipse.

## Klunkerkranich (Neukolln, Karl-Marx-Strasse 66, Neukolln Arcaden roof)

Cultural rooftop garden on the top parking deck of the Neukolln Arcaden mall, running since around 2013. Reach via the library/post elevator to the 5th floor then up the ramp. Free before 16:00; cover around 3 euros after 16:00, around 5 euros with live music. Bottle deposit 1 euro. Winter break January and February. Casual dress.

### Klunkerkranich Recurring Formats

- Indoor spaces: "Wohnzimmer" (main bar, Zum Fetten Finken canteen, concerts and jams, transforms into a dancefloor late and at weekends) and "Hinter den Alpen" (club on deck 7, warmer playful techno, house, downtempo, disco, plus Christmas market and flea markets).
- Live music and DJ nights alternate by day; the Sunday "Jazzy Berlin Jam Session" has over three hours of live jazz plus vinyl. Resident Nelken does warm-ups; series include Sauvage World's "Modern Cults" and UCR Salon (downtempo, organic grooves).
- No reservations; arrive early, especially summer weekends.

## RSO.BERLIN (Niederschoneweide, Schnellerstrasse 137)

On the grounds of the former Barenquell brewery in Schoneweide, inside Revier Sudost (the former Griessmuhle site). Two converted warehouse floors, Robus (cavernous) and Summe (intimate), plus open-air floors The Valley and OPAN, on a custom Kirsch Audio system (40kW, 48 channels, 153 drivers). Beer garden, light installations, panels. No photos in the club, only in the valley. Reach via S-Bahn S8, S9 or S45 to Schoneweide (4 stops from Ostkreuz), then a 5-minute walk, or Tram M27 / 21.

### RSO Recurring Formats and Festivals

- Home Again Club Festival: in-house multi-day festival by the Home Again house-music label (founder Nils Thabo Gelfort). The 2023 edition ran 19 to 22 May, billed as 65 hours of non-stop music with 49 artists across three stages; tickets from 17 euros, re-entry 5 euros. 2024 ran a roughly 100-hour edition (8 to 12 May) with over 50 artists. 2025 ran 28 May to 2 June across four stages with Peggy Gou, Solomun, Job Jobse, Helena Hauff, I Hate Models, Octo Octa, Logic1000, 999999999. 2024 tickets ran from 25 euros (day) to 49 euros (unlimited re-entry, five days). PLUR ethos, awareness team. A viral Marlon Hoffstadt set at The Valley made the open-air stage iconic.
- Wall2Wall Club Festival: recurring (third edition 2024) gathering local hosting crews across all floors. 2024 crews included GEGEN, Home Again, Plasma, PRXMTY, Raster, Selected, Self.Control., TILT, Veselka, We Are Not Alone, with a record market and RA panels. 2025 crews included HEISSS, Home Again, Plasma, Radiance, Raster, Selected, Self.Control., TILT, VERTX, We Are Not Alone.
- Gegen: the queer techno collective runs RSO editions (Gegen Aliens, futuristic dress code).

## Gretchen (Kreuzberg, former Prussian Dragoon stables)

Opened around 2011 in the 1854 former stables of a Prussian 1st Guards Dragoon Regiment; vaulted ceilings, cast-iron columns. Programs electronica, house, bass, dubstep, drum and bass, IDM, electro, glitch, experimental, hip hop, funk, soul, jazz and world. Won Berlin's Best Live Music Program award in 2023. Tickets via the box office; advance 8 to 12 euros for club nights, 20 to 40 euros for headline live shows.

### Gretchen Recurring Series

- Recycle: long-running drum and bass series, historically the first Saturday monthly, continuing the legacy of Icon Club (1996 to 2012) and earlier 90s Acud nights; hosts Hospitality Berlin and Neosignal showcases. DnB tempo typically 160 to 190 BPM.
- Impulse Basskultur: recurring bass-culture series.
- Weeknight live concerts span jazz, world, R&B, hip hop, indie and experimental.

## Lido (Kreuzberg, Cuvrystrasse 7, near Schlesisches Tor)

A 1950s/60s former cinema at Cuvrystrasse 7 in the Wrangelkiez near U1 Schlesisches Tor, reopened as a live and club venue in May 2006. Capacity around 550 to 600. Primarily a live concert venue (Maximo Park, Beatsteaks, CocoRosie) that also hosts club nights in the rear space.

- Balkanbeats: legendary monthly party run by Robert Soko (the original Berlin Balkanbeats since 1993).
- Other recurring nights: Soul Explosion, Kreuzberg Slam, Indie.Kollektiv, Rock AG, Kiss All Hipster.

## Loophole (Neukolln, Boddinstrasse 60) -- concerts banned July 2025

Former brothel at Boddinstrasse 60, opened around 2009 to 2011; ground floor plus a sweaty basement. Home of the RUFREAKTOR collective. Programmed experimental noise and leftfield electronica plus eclectic club nights, live concerts, exhibitions, drag, a free Wednesday film club, live drawing, karaoke, English-language comedy and queer parties. On 4 July 2025 Loophole was permanently banned from hosting concerts at Boddinstrasse after a police and Ordnungsamt raid; the collective announced closure with a farewell party.

## Humboldthain Club (Gesundbrunnen, S-Bahnhof Humboldthain, Hochstrasse 46)

In the S-Bahnhof Humboldthain at Hochstrasse 46 on the Wedding/Gesundbrunnen border since 2013; operator Ludwig Eben calls it "the small ugly sister of Berghain." Local-leaning underground electronic and techno; supports local artists and collectives (bbymeister, 5euroGoldi, GEN97, HiHat). The recurring queer party "Cuddles" (proggy house, acid, steamy techno) is hosted here. Under active development threat: a Luxembourg investor planned around 120 micro-apartments adjacent (later switched to a hotel); the club's petition gathered more than 10,000 signatures; on 19 March 2025 Mitte's district assembly passed a motion instructing the district office to review all current and future planning applications near the club. The operator warns the club could end after 2028 without a sensible lease renewal.

## Neighbourhood Deep-Dives

### Friedrichshain (Ostkreuz Corridor, Warschauer Strasse, RAW-Gelande, Holzmarkt)

Dense club corridor and the heart of nightlife tourism. Ostkreuz is the key S-Bahn interchange serving ://about blank (Markgrafendamm 24c) and the former Wilde Renate (Alt-Stralau). Warschauer Strasse and the RAW-Gelande cluster bars, clubs and street food. Holzmarkt 25 on the Spree hosts Kater and the Morchenpark; the East Side Gallery is nearby. Gentrifying fast (Mediaspree towers, Mercedes-Benz Arena) but still gritty around the tracks. Affordable bars and food across from Ostkreuz for pre- and after-club. Night transport: S-Bahn ring and radials, U1/U3 to Warschauer Strasse, trams.

### Kreuzberg (Schlesisches Tor / Flutgraben Cluster, Moritzplatz, Kottbusser Tor)

Classic alternative Kreuzberg. Schlesisches Tor (U1) anchors the Wrangelkiez with Lido (Cuvrystrasse 7) and access toward the Flutgraben cluster (Club der Visionaere, Hoppetosse, Birgit und Bier). Moritzplatz (U8) serves Ritter Butzke (Ritterstrasse 26); Gretchen is a short way south. Kottbusser Tor (U1/U8) is the rowdy late-night core with kebab and falafel for after-club food. Vibrant, diverse, partly gentrified; stay aware around Kotti late. Night transport: U1 and U8 (24-hour weekends), night buses.

### Treptow (Club der Visionaere Area, Arena Complex, Baumgarteninsel)

Greener and calmer. The Flutgraben where the Landwehrkanal meets the Spree hosts Club der Visionaere and Hoppetosse, part of the Arena Berlin complex (Eichenstrasse). Treptower Park is adjacent for daytime recovery. Lower-key than central Friedrichshain. Reach via S-Bahn Treptower Park or walk from Schlesisches Tor across the river.

### Treptow-Rummelsburg (Sisyphos)

Industrial, semi-isolated pocket in Rummelsburger Bucht, Lichtenberg. Sisyphos sits in the old dog-biscuit factory roughly 2 km east of Ostkreuz. Little around it, which suits the self-contained festival feel. Walk from Ostkreuz (about 25 minutes), Tram 21 to Gustav-Holzmann-Strasse, or taxi (about 10 euros).

### Schoneweide / Barenquell (RSO)

Far southeast in Niederschoneweide. RSO.BERLIN occupies the former Barenquell brewery on Schnellerstrasse, the post-Griessmuhle Revier Sudost site, by the river. Post-industrial, spread out, less touristy. Reach via S-Bahn S8, S9 or S45 to Schoneweide (4 stops from Ostkreuz, 3 from Neukolln) then a 5-minute walk, or Tram M27 / 21.

### Prenzlauer Berg / Pankow (Anomalie, Humboldthain)

Prenzlauer Berg is heavily gentrified, family-friendly and cafe-dense, with summer beer gardens (Prater Garten, FaF at Volkspark Friedrichshain nearby). Anomalie Art Club is at Storkower Strasse 123. Humboldthain Club is in the S-Bahnhof Humboldthain at Hochstrasse 46 on the Wedding/Gesundbrunnen edge, grittier than the leafy core. Good restaurants near Prater (60 Seconds to Napoli, 100 Gramm Bar to 03:00). Night transport: trams M10 and others, S-Bahn (Humboldthain, Gesundbrunnen), buses.

### Neukolln (Klunkerkranich, Loophole, Loftus Hall, former Griessmuhle)

Young, international, alternative, fast-gentrifying former working-class and Turkish-heritage district. Klunkerkranich is on the Neukolln Arcaden roof (Karl-Marx-Strasse 66, Rathaus Neukolln on U7/U17). Loophole (Boddinstrasse 60, U8 Boddinstrasse) ran experimental and queer nights (concerts banned July 2025). Loftus Hall housed the basement Bertrams where Herrensauna began. The former Griessmuhle site continues as Revier Sudost (RSO) further out. Maybachufer canal and Weserstrasse bars are good for pre- and post-club. Night transport: U7, U8, U7/U17, night buses.

### Mitte (Tresor / Kraftwerk / KitKat Corridor on Kopenicker Strasse)

The Kopenicker Strasse axis holds Kraftwerk Berlin (Tresor at no. 70, OHM in the same complex) and KitKatClub (no. 76), close to Heinrich-Heine-Strasse (U8) and Ostbahnhof. A mix of new development and surviving industrial sites along the Spree. Reach via U8 Heinrich-Heine-Strasse or S-Bahn Ostbahnhof. A destination corridor, not a wander.

### Alt-Stralau (Renate, Else)

The Rummelsburg Bay peninsula where Friedrichshain, Treptow and the water meet, by the Elsenbrucke. Former Wilde Renate (Alt-Stralau 70) and Else (An den Treptowers 10, with the Hansel and Brezel biergarten) sit here on the Spree. Quiet, watery, residential-edged. Tram 21 runs along the peninsula; nearby Treptower Park and Ostkreuz for transfers.
