# Salon zur Wilden Renate, Full Profile

## Basic facts

- Address: Alt-Stralau 70, 10245 Berlin (Friedrichshain, Alt-Stralau peninsula on the Spree)
- Contact: info@renate.cc (general), community@renate.cc, lost@renate.cc, studio@renate.cc
- Phone: (+49) 3025041426
- Website: renate.cc

## Opening hours

- Regular schedule: Thursday to Saturday, 23:00 to 09:00
- Garden opens earlier: Fridays from 16:00, Saturdays from 14:00 (free entry during garden hours)

## Rooms and spaces

- BLACK room: harder, deeper techno and house
- GREEN room: house, disco, electronica
- RED room: varied, often hosting guests and takeovers
- Garden (Garten): outdoor space open Fri from 4pm, Sat from 2pm with free entry during daytime hours; used for open airs, concerts, workshops, vinyl markets, and community events

## Door policy

- Age restriction: 21 and over with valid government-issued ID
- Entrance: roughly 20 euros including coat check; price varies by event
- Ticketing: via Resident Advisor or at the door
- Important note: an RA ticket does not guarantee entry; purchased tickets are refunded if turned away at the door
- Payment: fully cashless; no cash accepted
- Re-entry: stamp or wristband at exit

## Music

House, techno, disco, electro; the programme spans deep house and slower grooves on some nights through harder techno on others.

## Resident DJs

Ady Toledano, Amy Dabbs, Frozilla, Johannes Albert, Juan Ramos, VIO PRG

## Regular programming patterns

- Renate Klubnacht: the core weekly format, typically Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights, each room programmed by a different crew or promoter
- Renate Open Air and Garden: Fridays from 16:00 and Saturdays from 14:00, free entry; ranges from concerts to DJ sets, tarot readings, vinyl markets, workshops
- House of Lunacy at Renate: Lunacy Berlin's sex-positive chapter nights (the Renate building has the labyrinthine apartment-maze layout suited to these events); adults-only, consent-first, BLOOM and DECAY editions documented for 2026
- Manolita Open Air: garden event series with tapas, tarot, community DJs
- Radio Radio x Renate: occasional takeovers by the iconic Amsterdam house club (20-hour format)
- Petrified Phoenix: periodic full-venue takeover with drag programming in the garden

## Venue character

Multi-room venue in a former tenement building with maze-like rooms and a distinct house-party-gone-large feel. Each room is programmed independently by a different crew or promoter on the same night. This is the distinguishing feature of Renate: you are not going to see one headliner fill a single room; you are choosing which collective to follow.

The Alt-Stralau location sits on a peninsula between the Spree and the Rummelsburger See, away from the main Friedrichshain club strip. This isolation contributes to the self-contained community atmosphere. It is a 10-15 minute walk from Ostkreuz S-Bahn station.

## Code of conduct

Renate describes itself as a safe space for any ethnicity, gender or sexual orientation. Racism, sexism and homophobia result in removal. An awareness team is present at events. No photography is enforced strictly.

## What to expect / how to prepare

Renate is more relaxed on dress code than Berghain or KitKat. No strict aesthetic enforced; the crowd tends toward the eclectically dressed rather than all-black. The cashless policy is firm: no cash at all. Bring a card that works abroad (Visa/Mastercard). The RA-ticket-does-not-guarantee-entry clause means popular nights sell tickets but still allow the door team to curate. Arriving before midnight on Friday and before 2am on Saturday tends to reduce door uncertainty. The garden events are among the best free outdoor programming in Berlin in summer.
