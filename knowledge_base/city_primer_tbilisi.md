# Tbilisi Electronic Music Scene

Tbilisi emerged as one of Europe's most exciting electronic music cities in the 2010s, with politically charged techno culture born from Georgia's post-Soviet freedom struggles. Bassiani and Khidi became global destinations for hard, hypnotic techno while representing LGBTQ+ rights in a conservative society. The scene's intensity and political consciousness make it unique.

## Key Venues
- **Bassiani**: Legendary club under Dinamo Stadium, dark industrial space, hard hypnotic techno (135-145 BPM), global pilgrimage site, politically active (We Dance Together movement). Police raids in 2018 led to mass protests.
- **Khidi**: Raw warehouse space, equally serious, hard techno and industrial sounds, marathon sessions.
- **Mtkvarze**: Electronic music venue by the Mtkvari River.
- **Cafe Gallery**: Art space hosting electronic nights.

## Sound and Vibe
Hard, hypnotic, relentless techno (135-145+ BPM), minimal breaks, physical intensity. The scene is politically conscious - clubs represent freedom, LGBTQ+ rights, and resistance to conservatism. Georgians party hard, drink chacha (grape spirit), and embrace radical politics. Cheap compared to Western Europe. The scene punches far above the city's size.
