# Prague Electronic Music Scene

Prague's affordable costs, beautiful architecture, and growing underground scene attract DJs and ravers from across Europe. The city balances tourist-heavy center with serious underground clubs in industrial districts. Strong techno and house scenes with quality bookings and local talent.

## Key Venues
- **Ankali**: Multi-room industrial club, serious bookings, Funktion-One sound, marathon sessions (Friday-Sunday).
- **Fuchs2**: Former factory, industrial setting, techno and electro, underground vibe.
- **Cross Club**: Unique industrial steampunk aesthetic, three floors, diverse electronic bookings.
- **Roxy**: Historic venue, multiple rooms, NoD Experiment nights (legendary all-nighters).
- **Hivern**: Outdoor summer venue.

## Sound and Vibe
Techno-focused with strong minimal and hypnotic techno scenes. Affordable (10-15 EUR entry, cheap drinks), which attracts international party tourists but also maintains serious local scene. Less strict door policies than Berlin but quality-conscious. Strong connection to German and Austrian scenes.
