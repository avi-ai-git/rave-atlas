# Rome Electronic Music Scene

Rome's ancient architecture contrasts with its growing underground techno scene. Industrial venues in former factories host serious techno while beach clubs at Fregene and Ostia offer summer party alternatives. The scene is smaller than Milan but fiercely independent with quality bookings.

## Key Venues
- **Goa Club**: Long-running techno institution, dark industrial space, serious bookings, marathon sessions.
- **Rashomon**: Underground club, quality techno and house, intimate setting.
- **Circolo Illuminati**: Multi-level venue, diverse bookings from house to techno.
- **Lanificio 159**: Former wool factory, cultural space hosting electronic nights.

## Sound and Vibe
Techno-focused with Italian melodic house influences. Smaller scene than Milan or Bologna but passionate local community. Romans party late (arrive after 2am) and clubs often run until dawn. Affordable compared to northern Europe. Summer sees beach club season at Fregene (30 min from center).
