# Leipzig Electronic Music Scene

Leipzig is Berlin's underground sibling - a growing techno hub with a DIY ethos, affordable rents, and a serious local community. The city's post-industrial spaces host raw techno nights with international bookings. Closer to Berlin than most think (45 min train), but distinct musical identity.

## Key Venues
- **IFZ (Institut für Zukunft)**: Closest thing to Berghain in Leipzig, industrial space in former factory, multiple rooms, serious techno programming, international bookings.
- **elipamanoke**: "rave hard, be nice" - underground electronic music across multiple genres, two floors, techno/house/hard house, local-focused nights, Fridays chill, from trance to hardbounce.
- **Sky Club**: Since 2009 Leipzig's first address for electronic music, 3 floors + open air, techno/house/minimal/hardtekk, regular national/international artists, rooftop + indoor, industrial underground vibe.
- **Distillery**: Former location, regular techno events.

## Sound and Vibe
Hard techno, industrial, house, eclectic electronic. More local-focused than Berlin, budget concerts mean fewer big names but strong resident DJs. Industrial spaces, raw atmosphere. Affordable (10-15 EUR entry). Less touristy than Berlin. Serious music crowd.
