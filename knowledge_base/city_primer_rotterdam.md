# Rotterdam Electronic Music Scene

Rotterdam is Holland's raw techno city - underground bunker clubs, industrial grandeur, and cutting-edge electronic music. Perron is undisputed cornerstone (former railway arch, tagline "lots of smoke, little light", graffiti walls). Maassilo is colossal former grain silo. Dutch techno scene, serious underground.

## Key Venues
- **Perron**: Rotterdam's premier techno institution & rave hub, former railway arch, "lots of smoke, little light", graffiti walls, cutting-edge techno/deep house/electronic, weekend 23:59 open.
- **Maassilo**: Industrial grandeur & massive techno events, colossal former grain silo, architectural marvel, large-scale techno events/festivals.
- **Annabel**: Live acts & diverse electronic sounds, pop-podium, techno/drum & bass/dubstep.
- **Toffler**: Intimate basement & driving techno beats, former pedestrian tunnel turned techno club, neon ceiling, intimate basement.

## Sound and Vibe
Raw techno, underground bunker, cutting-edge electronic. Perron classic underground. Maassilo industrial grandeur. Serious Dutch techno scene, less touristy than Amsterdam. Affordable (10-20 EUR). Industrial architecture (railway arch, grain silo).
