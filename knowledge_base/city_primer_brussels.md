# Brussels Electronic Music Scene

Brussels' electronic heritage runs deep with Fuse opening in 1994 as one of Europe's oldest techno clubs. The city's central location between Paris, Amsterdam, and Cologne positions it as a crossroads for European electronic culture. Strong techno and electro scenes thrive in intimate venues.

## Key Venues
- **Fuse**: Legendary techno club at Rue Blaes 208, operating since 1994, one of Europe's oldest continuous electronic music venues, international DJ bookings every Saturday, industrial setting, 10-18 EUR entry.
- **C12**: Former industrial building, serious techno programming.
- **Recyclart**: Cultural space in former train station, hosts electronic nights.
- **Bloody Louis**: Small intimate venue, quality bookings.

## Sound and Vibe
Techno and electro dominate, influenced by Belgian New Beat heritage and German techno. Fuse maintains old-school rave ethos with accessible door policy but tourist-heavy crowds. Smaller venues offer more intimate experiences. Central European location means cheap travel for ravers from neighboring countries.
