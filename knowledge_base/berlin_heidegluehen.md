# Heideglühen — Full Profile

## Basic facts

- Name: Heideglühen (meaning roughly "heath glow" or "moorland glow")
- Location: Beusselstrasse area, Berlin-Mitte / Moabit border (northwest Berlin)
- Coordinates: 52°32'17.1"N 13°19'41.6"E
- Website: heidegluehen.berlin
- Age policy: strictly 21 and over; ID required

## How to get there

- S-Bahn: S41 or S42 ring line to Beusselstrasse
- Bus: lines 106, 123, N26 to Seestrasse/Beusselstrasse
- Tram: 50, M13 to Virchow-Klinikum

## Format and character

Heideglühen is a recurring outdoor-linked club event in northwest Berlin that does not follow the central techno-club template. Key facts:

- Events run from noon (12:00) on Saturday through to Sunday night (typically 22:00 for marathon editions, 06:00 for standard ones)
- The 34-hour "Doppelheide" editions are their flagship format: 34 continuous hours with a rotating cast
- Numbered event series (Heideglühen #18, #19, etc.) — not named themed nights; the numbers are the brand
- Cash desk (ticket office) closes three hours before the event ends; arrive before that point or you cannot enter
- No lineup announced far in advance; artists are listed closer to the date on the website and RA

## Music and booking style

Electronic music with a deep, hypnotic house and techno lean; bookings tend toward respected underground artists rather than commercial headliners. Documented artists from the June 2026 #18 edition include Marcel Dettmann, Roman Flügel, Antal, Isaac Carter, Shonky, Woody, Dr Banana, Violetta, Lucas Depta, Forsberg, Thilini.

The booking philosophy: a full arc from early afternoon chill to late-night intensity, mirroring the progression of a long outdoor festival more than a standard club night.

## What to know before going

Heideglühen is not in the usual Friedrichshain-Kreuzberg-Neukölln club corridor; it sits in northwest Berlin, closer to Moabit and Wedding. This is intentional: it is a slightly removed, self-contained space. Arriving at noon on Saturday is perfectly normal; that is when the event starts. The marathon format means many people arrive mid-afternoon for a proper day-night-morning arc. Dress for weather that can change over 20+ hours. Outdoor elements mean layers are sensible. The numbered series identity means there is no "I am going to see X" — you are going to Heideglühen as an experience, not to chase one artist.
