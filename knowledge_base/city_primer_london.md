# London Electronic Music Scene

London birthed UK garage, dubstep, and drum & bass while maintaining world-class house and techno scenes. The city's musical diversity is unmatched globally - from bass music in Peckham to techno in Elephant & Castle. Strict licensing, high costs, and venue closures challenge the scene, but quality and innovation persist.

## Key Venues
- **Fabric**: Iconic three-room club in Farringdon, Bodysonic dancefloor (bass through the floor), world-renowned bookings. Room 1 (techno), Room 2 (DnB/bass), Room 3 (house).
- **Printworks**: Former printing factory in Canada Water (closed 2023 but reopening), massive industrial space, state-of-the-art production.
- **Corsica Studios**: Railway arches in Elephant & Castle, two rooms, serious bookings, underground vibe.
- **FOLD**: Purpose-built club in Canning Town, Funktion-One system, marathon sessions.
- **Phonox**: Brixton club, intimate, quality house and techno.
- **Ministry of Sound**: Legendary since 1991, more commercial but historic significance.

## Sound and Vibe
UK garage, dubstep, grime, and drum & bass originated here, maintaining strong bass music culture alongside techno and house. Diverse scenes by area: Peckham (bass), Shoreditch (house/techno), South London (grime/dubstep). Licensing restrictions mean many clubs close at 6am. Expensive (£20-40 entry, £6+ drinks).
