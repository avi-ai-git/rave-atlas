# Paris Electronic Music Scene

Paris blends French touch heritage (Daft Punk, Justice) with serious techno and house culture. The city's historic ballroom venues and Seine-side clubs create elegant settings for underground music. Strong local scene with quality bookings and growing recognition on the global circuit.

## Key Venues
- **Concrete**: Legendary club on a boat on the Seine, closed 2020 but reopened in new location, marathon sessions, serious bookings.
- **Rex Club**: Historic venue since 1988, exceptional sound system, techno and house institution, Laurent Garnier's home club.
- **La Station - Gare des Mines**: Former rail warehouse, raw industrial space, quality techno.
- **Dehors Brut**: Outdoor summer venue in Canal de l'Ourcq, open-air parties May-September.
- **Badaboum**: Floating club, diverse bookings, techno and bass music.

## Sound and Vibe
French touch legacy influences melodic electronic sounds, but hardcore techno and hard bass scenes also strong. Parisian elegance meets underground grit. Door policies exist but less strict than Berlin. Smoking culture and late-night cafes extend the party beyond clubs. Strong connection to Belgium and Netherlands scenes.
