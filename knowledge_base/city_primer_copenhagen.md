# Copenhagen Electronic Music Scene

Copenhagen's electronic scene combines Scandinavian sophistication with serious underground techno culture. Culture Box leads the city's reputation for quality bookings and serious sound systems. High costs reflect Danish prices but production quality and crowd knowledge justify the expense.

## Key Venues
- **Culture Box**: Copenhagen's flagship underground club at Kronprinsessegade 54, three intimate rooms, house and techno, Friday-Saturday 10pm-8am, attitude code enforced, established venue with global reputation.
- **Bakken**: Underground techno venue, intimate setting.
- **Jolene**: Bar and club hosting electronic nights.

## Sound and Vibe
House and techno with Scandinavian precision and quality. Smaller scene than Stockholm but dedicated. Very expensive (30+ EUR entry, 8-10 EUR drinks) reflecting Copenhagen costs. Serious crowd, knowledgeable about music. Strong connection to Berlin and Hamburg scenes.
