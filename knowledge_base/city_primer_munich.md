# Munich Electronic Music Scene

Munich is more than Oktoberfest - it has a fine club scene that established itself in recent years. Historic underground club heritage (Ultraschall, MMA) influenced generations, while current venues like Pimpernel and Blitz carry the legacy. Serious techno culture despite Bavarian tourist perception.

## Key Venues
- **Pimpernel**: Authentic music club with best sound, legendary since 1970, Glockenbachviertel center, open daily 10pm-6am, one of Munich's best techno addresses.
- **Blitz**: Going from strength to strength despite pandemic, community/hedonism/fun values, global techno language, local + international DJs, true techno hotspot.
- **Harry Klein (Rote Sonne)**: Maximilianspl. 5, Ultra Visuals Mega Basse, techno elektro, All Night Long events, queer climate justice parties.

## Sound and Vibe
Techno, house, electronic. Historical legacy (Ultraschall legendary). Current scene serious, community-focused, hedonistic fun. Compact venues, quality sound systems. Less touristy. Affordable (15-25 EUR entry). Strong local DJ community. International DJs regular.
