# Belgrade Electronic Music Scene

Belgrade's post-conflict transformation birthed a fierce underground techno scene in repurposed industrial spaces. Drugstore and KPTM lead a city-wide movement of warehouse parties, electronic music collectives, and Balkans-specific energy. The scene's rawness and affordability make it a hidden gem on the European circuit.

## Key Venues
- **Drugstore**: DIY cultural space in former slaughterhouse at Poankerova Street, brutal industrial interiors, intense atmosphere, hosts around 1000 people, techno temple.
- **KPTM (Klub Podzemnih Tehnologija i Muzike)**: Underground Technologies and Music Club, serious techno.
- **Hangar**: Warehouse venue with regular techno parties.
- **20/44**: Underground club, quality bookings.
- **Under, SKC Baza**: Alternative venues hosting electronic nights.

## Sound and Vibe
Hard techno, industrial, and Balkan energy. Drugstore compared to NYC underground clubs for intensity. Very affordable (5-10 EUR entry, cheap drinks). Ravers are passionate, welcoming, and serious about music. Growing reputation draws international DJs. Serbian nightlife culture means late arrivals (2-3am) and all-night sessions.
