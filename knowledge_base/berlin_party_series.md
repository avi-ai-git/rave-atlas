# Berlin Recurring Party Series

## Why party series matter
Locals navigate Berlin's nightlife by series, not by venue. Knowing that Homopatik runs at about blank every first Saturday, or that Snax happens at Berghain twice a year, is more useful than knowing the venue exists. A recurring series means a consistent crowd, a consistent selector and sound, and a reliable benchmark for what you are walking into.

## Berghain / Panorama Bar recurring series

### Klubnacht
- The main Berghain weekend session. Starts at 23:59 Saturday (one minute before midnight), runs without stopping until Monday morning, sometimes longer on public holidays. Does not start on Friday.
- Three floors active: Berghain (hard, industrial techno 130 to 138 BPM), Panorama Bar upstairs (house, disco, Italo), Saule (experimental, leftfield electronic).
- Running order posted Thursday at noon on berghain.berlin. Always check before queuing.
- The Panorama Bar sunrise window (around 6am to 9am Sunday) is a ritual; natural light hits the dancefloor as the crowd peaks.
- Sunday afternoon is prized: the crowd has self-selected, the queue is shorter, and the garden opens in warmer months.

### Lab.oratory
- Men-only gay fetish and sex club in the Berghain basement.
- Runs most Friday and Saturday nights from around 22:00 or 23:00; doors locked at midnight (no entry after).
- Entry roughly 10 to 14 euros cash. Strict no-photo and no-cologne norms.
- Techno soundtrack; low lighting; dark rooms. Dress code: fetish or nude. Street clothes refused.
- A direct lineage from the early 1990s Snax parties in the Reichsbahnbunker before Berghain existed.

### Snax
- Men-only gay fetish party, descended directly from the 1990s Snax series. Now runs twice a year at Berghain, combining the main floor with Lab.oratory into one large queer fetish space.
- Edition 1: Snax Club, Easter weekend (the original, week-long fetish week in Berlin, with Snax as the centrepiece event).
- Edition 2: FC Snax United, late November or early December, with a sportswear theme (started 2011).
- Dress code: strict fetish only, more so than a standard Berghain night. Some accounts suggest street clothes are entirely refused.
- Programming on both floors for 24 plus hours; key Berghain residents and international queer techno acts.

### Saule programming
- The third floor inside Berghain (opened March 2017), smaller and positioned under the main floor.
- Programs darker, more experimental and leftfield electronic: noise, industrial, ambient, experimental techno.
- Often booked independently of the Klubnacht main lineup; check RA for the Saule schedule separately.

## about blank recurring series

### Homopatik
- Monthly queer party at about blank, one of Berlin's oldest recurring gay nights.
- Focus: queer community, DJs and live acts, inclusive and non-commercial.

### Buttons
- Recurring queer party at about blank, long-running and community-anchored.

### Staub
- Eclectic recurring night at about blank with house, techno and experimental.

## KitKatClub recurring series

### CarneBall Bizarre
- The KitKat flagship Saturday night, the defining fetish-techno experience. Co-founder Kirsten Kruger selects at the door. Dress code strictly fetish or creative erotic; street dress is refused.
- Runs most Saturdays. Techno and hard techno soundtrack.

### Symbiotikka
- Wednesday night queer techno at KitKat. Lower capacity, more intimate, community-focused.

### Nachspiel
- Sunday afterhours at KitKat, picking up the crowd that has been out all weekend.

### Electric Monday
- Monday night, lighter entry requirements, a more accessible introduction to the KitKat environment.

### Piep Show
- A shorter recurring format, mixed crowd, tech house and techno.

## Gegen (itinerant queer party)
- A radical queer techno party, not tied to one venue permanently. Has run at KitKat, RSO, and other Berlin spaces.
- Known for a diverse, confrontational, politically charged queer experience and excellent bookings.

## Herrensauna (itinerant gay techno)
- A men-only queer techno party that has run at Tresor, about blank, and other venues.
- Focus on hard techno and high-quality bookings in a sweaty, stripped-back environment.

## Pornceptual
- Sex-positive, queer and straight mixed, fetish-forward party. Has run at various Berlin venues including RSO.
- Focus on consensual exploration and artistic performance alongside techno.

## Cocktail d'Amore (historic, Griessmuhle era)
- The legendary sex-positive marathon party at Griessmuhle, a Spree canal venue in Neukolln. Ran from around 2014 until Griessmuhle's eviction in February 2020.
- Mixed, queer-friendly, BDSM-positive; combined marathon techno (Friday to Monday) with a record shop, canteen, flea market and community ethos.
- Its closure became a symbol of Clubsterben and the end of an era of post-industrial Berlin club freedom.
- The team behind it (including David Ciura) regrouped and opened RSO in the Barenquell brewery in Schoneweide.

## Wax Treatment (itinerant)
- A long-running itinerant event series focused on rare groove, soul, hip-hop and electronic music, associated with Prins Thomas, Dj Harvey and international selectors. Runs at various Berlin venues per booking.

## Killekill (label and party series)
- The Berlin label and party brand of Fantastic Twins (Camille Gillot and Juliane Heinz). Hard EBM, industrial, club music.
- Killekill is both a record label (releases from Lena Willikens, Petra Flurr, and others) and a recurring party format, running at about blank and Tresor among others.

## Leisure System (label and party series)
- A Berlin-based label and collective for leftfield electronic, ambient and experimental club music.
- Party series associated with small venues and art spaces; bookings lean toward Berlin's experimental edges.

## Club der Visionaere specials
- Largely unscheduled; bookings confirmed only a week or two ahead. Ricardo Villalobos and Richie Hawtin have played unannounced. The point is spontaneity and regulars knowing to check.

## How to find out what is running
- RA (Resident Advisor, ra.co): the definitive Berlin event calendar. Filter by date, venue and genre.
- Berghain.berlin: Thursday noon lineup posts.
- Individual Instagram accounts for each series.
- Groove Magazine (Germany) and Resident Advisor features for historical and cultural context.
