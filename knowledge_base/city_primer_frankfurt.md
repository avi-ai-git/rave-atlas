# Frankfurt Electronic Music Scene

Frankfurt is Germany's financial capital but has serious underground techno scene rooted in 1990s electronic music history. Industrial outskirts, former factories, and intimate basement clubs host harder techno and experimental sounds. Robert Johnson (technically Offenbach) is global icon.

## Key Venues
- **Robert Johnson** (Offenbach, Frankfurt-adjacent): Global icon in electronic music, unparalleled sound system, minimalist aesthetic, intimate atmosphere, focuses purely on music + dancefloor experience, undisputed Frankfurt techno identity.
- **Tanzhaus West**: Long-standing beloved institution, raw industrial charm, diverse electronic programming, strong techno emphasis.
- **Freud**: Recent but rapidly impactful, basement intimate underground, immersive intense clubbing, hard techno + industrial sounds focus.
- **Silbergold**: Unique intimate clubbing in multi-story car park building, cozy alternative setting, central Frankfurt, eclectic (house/techno/jungle/electro).
- **Adam Riese**: Frankfurt's new electronic music address, techno/house/underground beats, EINXEINS party series.

## Sound and Vibe
Hard techno, industrial, minimal, house. Robert Johnson defines Frankfurt minimalist electronic identity. Underground basement clubs for harder sounds. Industrial charm. Serious crowd, music-focused. Affordable (15-25 EUR entry). Strong connection to Berlin and Cologne.
