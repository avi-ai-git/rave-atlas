# Berlin Venues with Minimal or No RA Presence

These venues run regular programming but do not consistently appear in Resident Advisor searches; discovering them requires going to their own websites.

## Fitzroy

- Address: Holzmarktstrasse 15-18, 10179 Berlin (near Jannowitzbrucke S/U-Bahn, Holzmarkt complex)
- Instagram: @fitzroyclub
- Website: fitzroy-berlin.de

Fitzroy sits within the Holzmarkt cultural complex on the Spree, the same broader area as Kater Blau. The venue focuses on techno, bass, club and dubstep, with a programming identity distinct from the larger Holzmarkt venues.

Recurring series at Fitzroy:
- Almost Always: 17-hour day-to-night parties spanning afternoon to early morning; documented artists include Peverelist, Ciel, re:ni, Biggabush (June 2026 edition)
- Low Ends and Explorers: party series dedicated to bass, club and dubstep in collaboration with the Explorers collective
- 0PUNKT: evening format (17:00-03:00) with mixed bookings including Handbag, MOLO, The Touch, Kei Zwang
- Simbiosis at Fitzroy: Sunday daytime parties (14:00-22:00), end-of-summer groove focus

June 2026 confirmed events: 6 Jun (Almost Always with Peverelist), 12 Jun (Low Ends and Explorers), 14 Jun (0PUNKT), 13 Sep (Simbiosis daytime).

An awareness policy is in place; details on their website under /about/#awareness-policies.

## Kater Blau (Kater Club)

- Address: Holzmarktstrasse 25, 10243 Berlin (Holzmarkt area, near Jannowitzbrucke)
- Contact: hello@katerclub.de, awareness@katerclub.de
- Label: Kiosk ID Label
- Lost and Found: Tuesdays 18:00-20:00

Kater Blau (now operating as Kater Club after the Bar25 and KaterHolzig lineage) is one of the longest-standing party spaces in the Holzmarkt complex. After Bar25 closed in 2010 for the East Side Gallery restoration and KaterHolzig ran 2010-2013, the current iteration occupies the permanent Holzmarkt 25 site.

Music: electronic music spanning house, techno, acid and disco. Rooms include HOPPER and ACID BOGEN as named floors, plus EXTRA and EXTRAEXTRA spaces.

Recurring formats:
- FOREVER 25: multi-day curated festival editions (weekend-long, four times a year), each curated by a different guest crew. 2026 editions curated by Peter Schumann and miAs (June), Franca and Mimi Love (September), Enorm in Form (October), Mila Stern and Esther Silex (November/December)
- Katergarten: free summer evening open airs (17:00-22:00) on weekday evenings in the outdoor garden space

Radio streams: HOPPER channel and ACIDBOGEN channel (available via website).

Character: The Bar25 and KaterHolzig lineage brought an ethos of long-duration communal parties, art installations, and a more playful, less austere vibe than the harder Berlin techno spaces. Kater maintains this DNA. The crowd is typically mixed, internationally oriented, and less strictly in the Berlin-all-black aesthetic than Berghain.

Dress: expressive, eclectic; the Bar25 aesthetic of layered, bohemian or festival-wear is still the cultural reference. No single correct look; showing personality and intentionality is read better than generic clubwear.

## Lunacy Berlin and House of Lunacy

- Contact: info@lunacyberlin.com
- Website: lunacyberlin.com
- Social: Telegram for updates (recommended for sale announcements)

Lunacy Berlin is the collective and production company behind House of Lunacy, describing itself as "a constellation of many different events, artistic themes and experiences merging together to create a luniverse of creativity, sensual exploration and experimentation." It is connected to the House of Red Doors concept.

Events:
- House of Lunacy: the flagship series; chapter nights held at Renate (confirmed 2026 editions). Each chapter has a theme (BLOOM, DECAY documented for 2026). Adults-only, sex-positive, consent-centric. The Renate building's maze-like apartment rooms suit the immersive, intimate party format.
- The Village Festival: larger outdoor or multi-venue festival edition (details TBA for 2026).

House of Lunacy sits in the same tradition as Pornceptual and Gegen in being a sex-positive queer-adjacent party, but with more emphasis on art installation, theatrical theming and a mixed-gender (rather than specifically men-only or women-only) crowd.

## Lokschuppen Berlin

- Website: lokschuppen-berlin.com
- Location: Berlin (exact address on their website; not listed on RA)

Lokschuppen (German for "locomotive shed") is a venue and events space occupying a former industrial rail maintenance building. The name indicates repurposed rail infrastructure similar to the broader Berlin pattern of venue-in-found-space. It represents the category of Berlin club that exists primarily through its own channels and word of mouth rather than RA event listings. Check their website directly for current programming.

## How to find venues not on RA

Many of the best Berlin club nights exist primarily or entirely outside Resident Advisor. Strategies:

- Check venue websites directly (Renate, Sisyphos, Kater, Fitzroy, Heideglühen all have their own event calendars)
- Follow the venue or party's Instagram; lineup announcements often appear there first
- Join venue Telegram groups (Lunacy, Sisyphos and others use these for sale announcements)
- Berlin.de clubs A-Z: berlin.de/en/clubs/a-z lists official registered clubs including ones absent from RA
- RA still covers most medium-to-large Berlin events, but smaller collective nights and one-off events at non-RA venues will not appear there
