# Amsterdam Electronic Music Scene

Amsterdam anchors the Dutch electronic music scene with a blend of house, techno, and trance heritage. Home to Amsterdam Dance Event (ADE), the world's largest electronic music conference and festival, drawing 500,000+ visitors every October. The city's historic tolerance and canal-side venues create a unique party atmosphere.

## Key Venues
- **De School**: Former school building converted to club and restaurant, known for exceptional bookings and 24-hour weekend sessions, Funktion-One sound. Closed temporarily but influential.
- **Shelter**: Underground club beneath A'DAM Tower in Noord, dark industrial space, serious techno and house.
- **Radion**: Multi-room venue with outdoor terrace, diverse bookings from techno to electro.
- **Garage Noord**: Warehouse space in Noord, raw industrial setting.
- **Paradiso**: Historic church converted to venue, hosts electronic nights alongside live music.

## Sound and Vibe
Dutch house heritage (early 1990s), strong minimal and deep house scenes, and growing hard techno presence. More accessible door policies than Berlin but quality-focused programming. ADE week (October) sees the city transform into global electronic music hub with 400+ events.
