# Hamburg Electronic Music Scene

Hamburg is Germany's second-largest city with a fierce underground techno scene. Industrial waterfront, former war bunkers, and warehouse spaces create raw venues for serious techno. Uebel & Gefahrlich in a Hochbunker (air raid shelter) is world-renowned.

## Key Venues
- **Uebel & Gefahrlich**: 4th floor former Luftschutzbunker (air raid shelter) on Heiligengeistfeld/Feldstrasse 66, one of Germany's most famous clubs, two rooms (Ballsaal + Turmzimmer), capacity ~1000, techno to 1920s jazz, decorated warm interiors, award-winning.
- **Sudpol**: Techno club, regular underground events.
- **Fundbureau**: Underground techno venue, serious bookings.
- **Druckkammer**: Intimate techno space, local and international DJs.
- **Docks**: Venue hosting electronic nights.
- **Hafenklang**: Music venue with electronic programming.

## Sound and Vibe
Hard techno, industrial, house. Uebel & Gefahrlich world-renowned for techno parties. Industrial bunker setting, raw atmosphere, serious crowd. Less touristy than Berlin. Affordable (15-25 EUR entry). Strong local DJ community.
