# Berlin Clubs A-Z — Official Directory with Links

The Berlin Senate's official club directory (berlin.de/en/clubs/a-z) is the authoritative registry of every formally registered club in Berlin. Below is each club with its street address, official website, and official berlin.de detail page. This is the reference for finding event listings beyond Resident Advisor, since many of these venues publish their programme only on their own website or Instagram.

When a user asks where to find a club's events, give them the official website first (it has the real, current programme), and the berlin.de page as a backup. Resident Advisor covers only a subset of these venues.

## The Koepenicker Strasse techno corridor (Mitte/Kreuzberg)

- Berghain — Am Wriezener Bahnhof, 10243 Berlin. Official website: berghain.berlin (programme at berghain.berlin/de/program/). The flagship Berlin techno institution; Panorama Bar, Saule, Lab.oratory.
- Tresor — Koepenicker Str. 70, 10179 Berlin. Official website: tresorberlin.com (programme at tresorberlin.com/program). Founding Berlin techno venue since 1991; the vault, Globus upstairs.
- OHM — Koepenicker Str. 70, 10179 Berlin. Official website: ohmberlin.com (events at ohmberlin.com/upcoming). Experimental electronic in the old Kraftwerk battery room, part of the Tresor complex.
- KitKatClub — Koepenicker Str. 76, 10179 Berlin. Official website: kitkatclub.org. Fetish and sex-positive techno; CarneBall Bizarre on Saturdays, strict dress code.

## Spree-side and canal open-air venues (Friedrichshain/Treptow/Kreuzberg)

- Kater Blau — Holzmarktstr. 25, 10243 Berlin. Official websites: katerblau.de and katerclub.de. Bar25 lineage on the Spree; house, techno, confetti; FOREVER 25 and Katergarten series.
- Fitzroy — Holzmarktstr. 15-18, 10179 Berlin. Official website: fitzroy-berlin.de (events at fitzroy-berlin.de/events/). Instagram @fitzroyclub. Techno, bass, club, dubstep; Almost Always and Low Ends & Explorers series.
- Club der Visionaere — Am Flutgraben 1, 12435 Berlin. Official website: clubdervisionaere.com. Minimal house and techno on a canal-side wooden deck; intimate afterhours.
- Hoppetosse — Eichenstr. 4, 12435 Berlin. Official website: hoppetosse.berlin. Parties on a moored ship, three decks, house and techno with Spree views.
- Else — An den Treptowers 10, 12435 Berlin. Official berlin.de listing only. Seasonal open-air dancefloor on the Spree banks; house and techno.
- Birgit (und Bier) — Schleusenufer 3, 10997 Berlin. Official website: birgit.club. Open-air club and beer-garden on the canal; festival vibe, hosts SONAR safer-nightlife.
- RSO.Berlin — Schnellerstr. 137, 12439 Berlin. Official website: rso.berlin. Former Barenquell brewery in Schoeneweide; open-airs, Home Again festival, CTM programming.

## RAW-Gelaende cluster (all at Revaler Str. 99, Friedrichshain)

The RAW-Gelaende is a former railway-repair-yard culture site on Revaler Strasse hosting several venues at one address:

- Cassiopeia — cassiopeia-berlin.de. Multi-floor subculture venue in a former industrial hall.
- Badehaus — badehaus-berlin.com. Converted bathhouse; live acts and varied club nights.
- Astra Kulturhaus — astra-berlin.de. Big-stage concert and club venue, large capacity.
- Lokschuppen — lokschuppen-berlin.com (lokschuppen-berlin.com/2/). Driving-techno nights in a former locomotive shed.

## Larger, mixed-genre and queer venues

- Metropol — Nollendorfplatz 5, 10777 Berlin. Website: metropol-berlin.de (events at metropol-berlin.de/events). Historic 1,000-capacity theatre; hosts SchwuZ relaunch parties from 2026.
- SchwuZ — Rollbergstr. 26, 12053 Berlin. CLOSED since November 2025 (insolvency August 2025); the supporting association is relaunching parties at the Metropol. Germany's oldest and largest queer club.
- Lido — Cuvrystr. 7, 10997 Berlin. Website: lido-berlin.de. Indie, rock, pop in a historic Kreuzberg cinema-club; live concerts and themed parties.
- Yaam — An der Schillingbruecke 3, 10243 Berlin. Website: yaam.de. Afro-Caribbean beach club on the Spree; afrobeat, reggae, dancehall, hip-hop.
- Monarch — Skalitzer Str. 134, 10999 Berlin. Website: kottimonarch.de. Living-room-flair bar-club above Kottbusser Tor; techno to funky pop.
- Bi Nuu — Schlesisches Tor, 10997 Berlin. Website: binuu.de. Wide range from hardcore-punk concerts to electro parties.

## How to find current events for these venues

1. Official website first: most of these clubs publish their full, current programme on their own site (the website listed above). This is more complete and current than Resident Advisor for many venues.
2. Berlin.de detail page: berlin.de/en/clubs/a-z links to a detail page for every registered club, with the official site and address. This is the authoritative fallback.
3. Resident Advisor: covers roughly 40 to 60 percent of these venues with consistent event listings; use it for the bigger and more internationally-known clubs.
4. Instagram: many smaller collectives and one-off nights announce only on Instagram. There is no reliable automated way to read this; it must be checked manually.

The single best strategy for a complete picture of a given weekend is to combine Resident Advisor (for the major venues) with the official websites of the specific clubs you care about (for everything RA misses).
