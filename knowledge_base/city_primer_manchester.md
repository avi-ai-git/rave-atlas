# Manchester Electronic Music Scene

Manchester's electronic music heritage runs from acid house warehouse raves at Hacienda (1982-1997) through to today's bass-heavy underground. The city birthed Madchester, rave culture's UK crossover, and maintains fierce independence from London. Warehouse Project dominates autumn/winter with massive events while smaller clubs keep the underground alive.

## Key Venues
- **The Warehouse Project**: Seasonal (September-January) massive warehouse events at Depot Mayfield, major international bookings, 10,000+ capacity, defines Manchester's electronic calendar.
- **Hidden**: Intimate basement club, serious techno and house bookings.
- **YES**: Pink Room hosts quality electronic nights, diverse programming.
- **Gorilla**: Live venue that hosts electronic nights.
- **Factory**: New venue honoring Factory Records legacy.

## Sound and Vibe
Bass music (dubstep, grime, DnB) thrives alongside techno and house. Manchester's working-class roots and musical heritage create a serious, unpretentious scene. Warehouse Project brings global names but smaller venues maintain intimacy. Cheaper than London (£15-25 entry), friendly crowd. Strong local production talent and independent labels.
