# Glasgow Electronic Music Scene

Glasgow's electronic scene punches far above its size with Sub Club (opened 1987) as one of the UK's most respected techno institutions. The city's grit, independent spirit, and quality-over-hype ethos create a fiercely loyal community. Scots know their music and the dancefloors reflect that knowledge.

## Key Venues
- **Sub Club**: Legendary basement club since 1987, Berkley Suite sound system, Sub Culture nights (Saturday) are institution, intimate, sweaty, serious techno and house.
- **SWG3**: Warehouse complex with multiple rooms, Galvanizers hosts quality electronic bookings, Body Sync world's first energy-storing dancefloor (powers venue from dancing).
- **La Cheetah**: Underground club (closed but was influential).
- **Stereo**: Venue and bar hosting electronic nights.

## Sound and Vibe
Techno, house, and bass music with Scottish intensity. Sub Club's reputation draws international DJs who respect its legacy. Glaswegians are serious about music, no pretension, all about the sound. Affordable (£10-20 entry), friendly crowd, less touristy than Edinburgh. Strong local DJ and production community.
