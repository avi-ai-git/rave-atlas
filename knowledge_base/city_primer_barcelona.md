# Barcelona Electronic Music Scene

Barcelona balances beach club hedonism with serious underground techno culture. Primavera Sound, Sonar, and Off Week draw global crowds, but the local scene thrives year-round in industrial spaces and beachfront venues. The city's 24-hour summer energy and Mediterranean setting create a unique party environment.

## Key Venues
- **Moog**: Legendary small club in Gothic Quarter, dark basement, serious techno and electro since 1994.
- **Razzmatazz**: Massive five-room complex in Poble Nou, diverse bookings, The Loft room hosts top techno.
- **Sala Apolo**: Historic theater hosting Nasty Mondays (indie/electronic) and quality electronic nights.
- **Input**: Intimate club focusing on underground house and techno, quality bookings, serious crowd.
- **Pacha Barcelona**: Commercial but hosts quality nights during festival season.

## Sound and Vibe
Tech-house and melodic techno popular in beach clubs, harder techno in industrial venues. Off Week (June, during Sonar festival) sees warehouse parties across the city. Strong local DJ and producer community. Summer party season runs May-September with beach clubs and open-air events. More accessible than Berlin but can be touristy.
