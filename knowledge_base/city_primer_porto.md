# Porto Electronic Music Scene

Porto's electronic scene rivals Lisbon with serious techno culture in historic and industrial venues. The city's riverside setting and affordable costs attract quality bookings and create an intimate community. Gare Club is considered one of Portugal's best venues with programming from house to dark industrial techno.

## Key Venues
- **Gare Porto**: Porto's techno temple at 182 Rua du Madeira (near Sao Bento station), considered one of Portugal's best clubs, hosts everything from house to darkest techno, Friday/Saturday midnight-7am, affordable entry.
- **Industria Club**: Porto's oldest club (open since 1986, renovated with Funktion-One sound), gathering place for local and international DJs, exceptional acoustics.
- **Plano B**: Underground electronic venue.
- **Maus Habitos**: Cultural space hosting experimental electronic nights.

## Sound and Vibe
Techno-focused (industrial, hard techno, and hypnotic styles), with house and minimal scenes. More underground and serious than Lisbon's scene. Affordable, intimate venues, strong local community. Portuguese ravers are welcoming and music-focused. Connection to Spanish and French scenes.
