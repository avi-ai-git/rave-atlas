# Zurich Electronic Music Scene

Zurich embraced techno early in the 1990s and maintains a strong scene despite Switzerland's high costs. Street Parade (annual techno demonstration drawing 1 million visitors since 1992) showcases the city's commitment to electronic culture. Intimate clubs in industrial areas host quality bookings.

## Key Venues
- **Hive Club**: Geroldstrasse 5, one of Zurich's main electronic dance music venues, party through the night into morning.
- **Zukunft**: Underground techno club, serious bookings.
- **Supermarket**: Alternative venue hosting electronic nights.

## Sound and Vibe
Techno and house scenes with Swiss precision and quality. Street Parade (August) is massive annual techno event - "demonstration for love, freedom, generosity and tolerance" with 1 million attendees. Very expensive (Swiss prices: 25-35 CHF entry, expensive drinks). Serious crowd, less touristy than Berlin or Amsterdam. Strong connections to German and Italian scenes.
