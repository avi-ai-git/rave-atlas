# Lisbon Electronic Music Scene

Lisbon's hills, miradouros (viewpoints), and riverside venues create stunning settings for electronic music. The city's growing reputation draws international DJs while maintaining an intimate local scene. More affordable than Barcelona or Paris with a relaxed, friendly atmosphere.

## Key Venues
- **Lux Fragil**: Legendary club by the river, owned by actor John Malkovich, rooftop terrace, quality bookings, institution since 1998.
- **Village Underground Lisboa**: Converted buses and shipping containers, open-air venue, creative bookings.
- **Ministerium**: Multi-room warehouse club, serious techno and house.
- **Incognito**: Small underground club, quality techno.
- **Dock's Club**: Riverside venue, house and techno.

## Sound and Vibe
Tech-house, melodic techno, and Afro house are popular, influenced by Portugal's connection to Angola and Mozambique. Friendly door policies, affordable drinks, late-night culture blends with cafe and miradouro hangouts. Summer sees rooftop and outdoor parties with sunset views. More relaxed than Berlin but musically serious.
